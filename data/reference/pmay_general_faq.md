# PMAY — General FAQ

### What is PMAY?
PMAY (Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana) is the Government of India's flagship affordable housing scheme, launched to achieve "Housing for All" by providing financial assistance and interest subsidies to help economically weaker, low-income, and middle-income families build, buy, or improve a permanent (pucca) home.

### When was PMAY launched?
PMAY-Urban (PMAY-U) was launched in 2015. PMAY-Gramin (PMAY-G), the rural component, was launched in 2016, replacing the earlier Indira Awaas Yojana with a more transparent, data-driven framework.

### What are the two main components of PMAY?
- **PMAY-Urban (PMAY-U):** for applicants residing in urban/town areas, implemented by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs.
- **PMAY-Gramin (PMAY-G):** for applicants residing in rural areas, implemented by the Ministry of Rural Development.

### What is the main objective of PMAY?
The core objective is "Housing for All" — ensuring every eligible family has access to a permanent, pucca house with basic amenities, thereby improving living standards and promoting inclusive urban and rural development.

### What are the key benefits of PMAY?
- Financial assistance for constructing a new house, or interest subsidy for purchasing/constructing one through a home loan.
- Basic amenities support, such as access to toilets, electricity, LPG, and drinking water connections, often converged with other government schemes.
- Encouragement of home ownership in the name of women, or joint ownership between spouses.
- Improved living standards and social/economic security for low-income and vulnerable families.
- Priority for the homeless, manual scavengers, SC/ST households, minorities, persons with disabilities, and other vulnerable groups.

### Who is eligible for PMAY?
In general, an applicant is eligible if their household does not already own a pucca (permanent) house anywhere in India, they have not previously availed central government housing assistance, and their household income falls within the scheme's defined income categories. Exact criteria differ slightly between PMAY-U and PMAY-G, as described below.

### What are the income categories under PMAY-Urban?
PMAY-U classifies applicants into: Economically Weaker Section (EWS), Low Income Group (LIG), Middle Income Group I (MIG-I), and Middle Income Group II (MIG-II), with progressively higher annual household income limits. Since these limits are revised periodically, applicants should confirm current thresholds on the official PMAY portal rather than assume a fixed figure.

### Who is eligible for PMAY-Gramin?
PMAY-G targets rural households identified as houseless or living in kutcha (temporary/dilapidated) houses, based on deprivation criteria from the Socio-Economic Caste Census (SECC) and subsequent verified data (the Awaas+ list), with priority given to SC/ST households, manual scavengers, minorities, persons with disabilities, and other vulnerable groups.

### What is the Credit Linked Subsidy Scheme (CLSS)?
CLSS is a component of PMAY-Urban that provides an interest subsidy on home loans taken by eligible EWS, LIG, and MIG beneficiaries to buy, construct, or improve a house, reducing the effective interest burden on the loan.

### What is a "pucca house" and why does it matter for eligibility?
A pucca house is a permanent, all-weather dwelling built with durable materials (as opposed to a kutcha house made of temporary materials like mud or thatch). Not owning a pucca house anywhere in India is a core eligibility condition for PMAY, since the scheme targets households currently without adequate permanent housing.

### Can a family that has already received government housing assistance apply again?
No. A core eligibility condition is that the family must not have previously availed central assistance under any housing scheme from the Government of India or a state government, and must not have already received a PMAY subsidy.

### Is PMAY only for very poor families?
No. While it prioritises the Economically Weaker Section and Low-Income Groups, PMAY-Urban also extends interest-subsidy benefits to Middle-Income Groups (MIG-I and MIG-II) who do not own a pucca house, making it broader than a poverty-only scheme.

### How does someone apply for PMAY?
Applicants can apply online through the official PMAY portal or offline through a Common Service Centre (CSC), by filling in the application form with Aadhaar details and submitting the required documents; PMAY-G applications are typically processed through Gram Panchayats using the Awaas+ beneficiary list.

### What documents are typically required to apply for PMAY?
Commonly required documents include Aadhaar details, income proof, proof of not owning a pucca house, bank account details, and — depending on the sub-scheme — property-related documents such as an allotment letter, approved building plan, or valuation certificate. PMAY-G applicants may also need to link their MGNREGA job card and Swachh Bharat Mission registration, since house construction assistance under PMAY-G is often converged with these schemes.

### How is the house construction/purchase assistance funded?
PMAY is funded jointly by the central and state governments, with the exact cost-sharing ratio differing between PMAY-Urban and PMAY-Gramin, and between general-category and hilly/northeastern/special-category states.

### Who implements PMAY?
PMAY-Urban is implemented by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs in partnership with state governments and urban local bodies. PMAY-Gramin is implemented by the Ministry of Rural Development in partnership with state governments and Gram Panchayats.

### Does PMAY promote women's ownership of housing?
Yes. The scheme encourages allotment of the house in the name of the female head of the household, or joint ownership between husband and wife, as a way to strengthen women's property rights and financial inclusion.

### What basic amenities are linked with PMAY houses?
PMAY construction is often converged with other government schemes to ensure beneficiary houses have basic amenities such as a toilet (Swachh Bharat Mission), electricity connection (Saubhagya), LPG connection (Ujjwala Yojana), and drinking water access.

### How can an applicant check their PMAY application or beneficiary status?
Applicants can check their status online on the official PMAY-Urban or PMAY-Gramin portal using their application number, Aadhaar number, or registered mobile number, or by contacting their local urban local body or Gram Panchayat.

### Where can official, up-to-date PMAY information be checked?
The official PMAY-Urban portal (pmay-urban.gov.in) and the PMAY-Gramin portal (pmayg.nic.in), maintained respectively by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs and the Ministry of Rural Development, are the authoritative sources for current eligibility criteria, income limits, subsidy amounts, and application status.
