## Overview

Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY) is a Government of India housing scheme to
provide affordable housing to low and moderate-income households, launched in
June 2015 with a target of 2 crore (20 million) houses by 31 March 2022. It has
two components: **PMAY-Urban (PMAY-U)**, run by the Ministry of Housing and
Urban Affairs, and **PMAY-Gramin / Rural (PMAY-G, also PMAY-R)**, run by the
Ministry of Rural Development. PMAY is delivered alongside complementary
programmes — Swachh Bharat Mission (toilets), Saubhagya (electricity), Ujjwala
(LPG), drinking water, and Jan Dhan banking.

## History

Public housing in India began with post-Partition refugee rehabilitation. The
Village Housing Programme (1957, second Five-Year Plan) gave loans up to ₹5,000
per unit. The Indira Awas Yojana (IAY), launched by Prime Minister Rajiv Gandhi
in 1985, was the main rural housing scheme — initially for Scheduled Caste /
Scheduled Tribe and minority households, later extended to all Below Poverty
Line households. In 2016 IAY was restructured into PMAY-Gramin. Prime Minister
Narendra Modi launched PMAY in June 2015.

## The Scheme

### Features

For urban beneficiaries, PMAY provides an interest subsidy on housing loans
under the Credit-Linked Subsidy Scheme (CLSS): 6.5% for Economically Weaker
Sections (EWS) and Low-Income Groups (LIG), 4% for Middle Income Group I
(MIG-I), and 3% for MIG-II, for a tenure of 20 years. A commonly cited maximum
interest subsidy is about ₹2.67 lakh per house. Ground-floor units are
preferentially allotted to elderly and differently-abled beneficiaries. Houses
are typically owned by a woman of the household or jointly.

### Income Categories (Urban)

- **EWS:** annual household income up to ₹3 lakh
- **LIG:** ₹3 lakh to ₹6 lakh
- **MIG-I:** ₹6 lakh to ₹12 lakh
- **MIG-II:** ₹12 lakh to ₹18 lakh

Home renovation / improvement / self-construction loans under CLSS are limited to
the EWS and LIG categories.

### Eligibility Conditions (Urban CLSS)

- The beneficiary family must not own a pucca house anywhere in India in the
  name of any household member.
- The family must not have availed any central or state housing subsidy earlier.
- A maximum beneficiary age of about 70 years is applied to loan tenure
  calculations.

### Phases (Urban)

- **Phase 1:** April 2015 – March 2017, 100 cities
- **Phase 2:** April 2017 – March 2019, +200 cities
- **Phase 3:** April 2019 – March 2022, remaining cities

## Predecessor and Related Schemes

**Indira Awas Yojana (1985)** was among India's first centralised rural housing
schemes and was merged into PMAY-Gramin in 2016. **Rajiv Awas Yojana (RAY)** was
a centrally sponsored slum-redevelopment scheme (around 2013–14) aiming at a
"slum-free India"; its sanctioned units were subsumed into later urban housing
totals.

## Convergence

PMAY is coordinated with DigiLocker (identity/services), Jal Jeevan Mission
(household water), One Nation One Ration Card, Saubhagya (electrification),
Swachh Bharat Mission (toilets), and Ujjwala (LPG).

> Note on PMAY-Gramin specifics (from official framework documents, for
> triangulation): beneficiaries are identified from SECC 2011 housing-deprivation
> data plus the Awaas Plus survey, validated by the Gram Sabha and kept as a
> permanent wait list; unit assistance is higher for North-Eastern / hilly / IAP
> areas than for plain areas; minimum house size is 25 sq m; the unskilled
> labour component is met by MGNREGA convergence and the toilet by SBM-G
> convergence; funds are released in instalments by DBT to the beneficiary's
> account and tracked in AwaasSoft with geo-tagged photographs via the Awaas
> App. The precise rupee amounts and Centre:State ratios are in the SME
> reference docs.
